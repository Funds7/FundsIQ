When contacting us

To help us respond more effectively, please include:

  • The email address associated with your FundsIQ account.
  • A clear description of the problem or question.
  • The relevant page or feature where the issue occurred.
  • A screenshot when it helps explain the problem.
  • A transaction reference for relevant payment issues.

Security reminder: Never send your password, bank PIN, OTP, Firebase credentials, Paystack secret key or other private security information to anyone claiming to provide FundsIQ support.
