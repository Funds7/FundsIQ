Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about FundsIQ.

What is FundsIQ?

FundsIQ is an online learning platform that helps students study GST courses, practise CBT questions and monitor academic performance.

Who can use FundsIQ?

FundsIQ is designed primarily for university students who want digital tools for studying and examination practice.

What are CBT practice tests?

CBT practice tests simulate computer-based examinations, allowing students to practise answering questions in a digital examination environment.

Does FundsIQ have a GPA calculator?

Yes. FundsIQ includes an academic calculator designed to help students calculate GPA and CGPA.

Does FundsIQ have Premium?

Yes. FundsIQ offers a Premium option with additional features and an ad-free experience.

Is FundsIQ affiliated with my university?

FundsIQ is an independent student learning platform unless a specific institutional partnership is clearly stated.
